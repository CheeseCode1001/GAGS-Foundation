Our Story

A Journey of Hope and Purpose

GAGS Foundation was born out of a deep desire to address the challenges faced by widows in our communities. What started as a small community initiative has grown into a movement that touches thousands of lives across Nigeria.

We believe that every widow deserves to live with dignity, hope, and equal opportunities. Our programs are designed to provide holistic support — from emotional healing to economic empowerment — ensuring that no widow is left behind.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To create a world where widows live with dignity, hope, and equal opportunities. We envision communities where every widow is empowered to thrive, not just survive — where compassion drives action and no one is forgotten.

Our Mission

To empower widows through comprehensive support programs including counseling, education, skills acquisition, healthcare, and community care. We are committed to restoring hope, rebuilding lives, and fostering sustainable independence.
