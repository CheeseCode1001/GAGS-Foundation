Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible? +

Yes, GAGS Foundation is a registered non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You will receive a receipt for your records.

How is my donation used? +

70% of all donations go directly to programs and services. The remaining 30% covers emergency relief, operations, and community awareness campaigns.

Can I donate in-kind? +

Absolutely! We welcome donations of food, clothing, school supplies, and other essential items. Please contact us to arrange a drop-off or pickup.

Can I set up recurring donations? +

Yes, we encourage monthly giving! Contact us to set up a standing order or recurring bank transfer to support our ongoing programs.
