Questions

Partnership FAQs

What types of partnerships do you offer? +

We welcome various partnership models including financial partnerships, skills training providers, supply partnerships, volunteer organizations, and media partnerships. Each partnership is tailored to your organization's capabilities and interests.

What is the minimum commitment required? +

There is no fixed minimum commitment. Partnership terms are flexible and customized based on your organization's capacity. We work with startups to large corporations, tailoring each partnership accordingly.

How long does the application process take? +

We review all applications within 7 business days. After review, we'll contact you to discuss partnership opportunities and next steps. Complex partnerships may take longer to finalize.

Can we track the impact of our partnership? +

Yes! We provide detailed impact reports and quarterly updates showing exactly how your contribution is making a difference. You'll see the real outcomes of your partnership.

Do partnerships come with marketing opportunities? +

Absolutely. We feature our partners on our website, in newsletters, social media, and annual reports. We also co-host events and joint campaigns to maximize visibility for both organizations.

How can we expand our partnership over time? +

Partnerships are flexible and can grow with your organization. We have quarterly reviews to assess impact and explore new opportunities. Many of our partners have evolved their involvement over time.
