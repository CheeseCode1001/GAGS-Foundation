Our Programs & Services

Comprehensive support programs designed to empower widows and transform communities.

What We Offer

Programs That Transform Lives

Each program is carefully designed to address the unique challenges widows face, providing holistic support for lasting change.

Mental Health

Counseling & Emotional Support

Professional counseling services providing safe spaces for widows to heal, process grief, and rebuild emotional strength. Our trained counselors offer individual and group therapy sessions.

Empowerment

Skills Acquisition Training

Hands-on vocational training in tailoring, baking, hairdressing, computer literacy, and more. We equip widows with marketable skills for financial independence.

Finance

Financial Empowerment

Micro-loans, savings groups, and business mentorship to help widows start and grow small businesses. Building sustainable livelihoods one widow at a time.

Relief

Food & Clothing Support

Regular distribution of food packages, clothing, and essential supplies to widows and their families. Ensuring basic needs are met with dignity and care.

Education

Educational Support for Children

Scholarships, school supplies, and tutoring for children of widows. Every child deserves access to quality education regardless of circumstance.

Healthcare

Healthcare Assistance

Medical outreach, health screenings, and support for healthcare costs. Partnering with clinics to ensure widows receive quality medical attention.
